Chef's Depot

Design System

A complete, build-ready design language for turning Chef's Depot into a best-in-class commercial kitchen equipment store — built around your real brand: the Chef's Depot green, a gold membership accent, an elegant serif headline voice, and the glassmorphism hero from your approved top-of-home design.

It keeps the best structural patterns from your reference sites — the visual mega menu, category cards, side-filtered category pages and spec-rich product pages — but dresses them entirely in the Chef's Depot identity and wires in the trade-membership model (member vs RRP pricing, ex/inc GST, "Members save 10–25%") that the open-retail references don't have. Member benefit and savings cues are carried in gold, echoing the crown and stat figures in your hero.

What changed from v1 → v2
  • Palette rebuilt from the official logo + approved design: forest green #45844D (brand/header), jewel teal #00786F (primary actions), gold #E9B949 (membership/savings), warm near-black (trust bar/footer).
  • Serif headline voice (Fraunces) for hero & marketing, matching the approved design; IBM Plex Sans for all UI/body; IBM Plex Mono for SKUs/specs.
  • The real logo is used in the header, footer and throughout (white on green/dark; a green variant is provided for light backgrounds).
  • Hero rebuilt as the glassmorphism-over-kitchen-photo layout with the lead card, Member Benefits card and the gold stat card.
Foundations

Logo & Usage

The stacked "CHEFS / DEPOT — Culinary Supplies" wordmark is the primary brand lockup. It ships as transparent PNGs so it sits cleanly on any background.

Usage rules
  • White wordmark (chefs-depot-logo-white.png) on the green header, dark footer, and photographic hero backgrounds.
  • Green wordmark (chefs-depot-logo-green.png) on white / light surfaces (invoices, light pages, email).
  • Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the "CHEFS" cap height. Don't recolour, stretch, add shadows, or place the white version on light backgrounds.
  • Minimum header height ≈ 44–48px on desktop, ≈ 34px on mobile. Provide an SVG master to the dev for crispness at all sizes (PNG supplied as an interim).

Semantic rules
  • Teal 700 = the only "buy/go" button colour. Green 500/700 = brand surfaces (header, nav, brand fills) — not buttons, so the action stays distinct from the brand field.
  • Gold is reserved for everything membership & savings: Member Price badge, savings figures, stat numbers, crown, the membership left-border on the buy box. Users learn "gold = what membership gets me."
  • Rust appears only on Save% / clearance urgency — used sparingly so it keeps its punch.
  • Body text = Ink 900; secondary = Steel 500/700. AA contrast minimum everywhere.

Pricing & GST — the trade model

Every price exists in up to four states: member vs RRP × ex-GST vs inc-GST. The GST control mirrors your header's "Excluding GST" switch and is global; member pricing depends on membership state. Member savings are always shown in gold.

Global GST switch

Including GST

Tap to flip · persists in cookie/localStorage · re-renders all prices. Default Ex GST (trade).

Pricing logic to implement
  • Each product carries rrp, memberPrice, gstRate, optional clearancePrice. Derive every displayed figure — never store inc/ex separately.
  • GST: inc = round(ex × (1+gstRate)). State in a global store (cookie + context) so SSR and client agree; default Ex GST.
  • Member state from membership session: members see memberPrice (RRP struck, gold savings line); guests see RRP plus the gold "Join → pay $X" conversion line — the funnel into $14.95/mo.
  • One shared <Price/> renders on cards, PDP and cart so states never diverge. Show savings as both $ and %.

React component inventory

Definition of done — per component
  • Uses only token variables — no hard-coded colours/sizes.
  • Correct in all states: member/guest, ex/inc GST, in-stock/low/out, sale/full.
  • Responsive at documented breakpoints; touch targets ≥44px; SSR-safe (no GST/member flicker).
  • Accessible: semantic markup, keyboard operable (mega menu, filters, tabs, qty, GST switch), visible focus, AA contrast.
  • Logo uses the supplied transparent assets (request an SVG master); images via next/image; schema emitted where relevant.

Phasing

1 · Foundation: tokens, fonts, logo, Button/Badge/Price/GST, header + mega menu, hero, trust bar, footer. 2 · Shop: product card, category page + filters, product page. 3 · Merchandising: homepage blocks, clearance/featured rows, brand pages, membership funnel, schema + performance.
